# Nodetrail brand kit

Official Nodetrail mark, coin, lockups, and profile images.

**Scan first. Ape different.™**

This is the F5a mark in the official color scheme (orange and gold with an electric-blue edge). Use these files as they are. For the product, go to [nodetrail.io](https://nodetrail.io). For official links and safety, go to [nodetrail.io/security](https://nodetrail.io/security).

## Which file to use

- Site, app, wallet, and favicon icons: the glow coin (`svg/nodetrail-coin.svg` or the PNG size you need)
- Print, docs, and UI on a controlled background: the flat color mark (`svg/nodetrail-mark.svg`)
- Dark headers: `svg/nodetrail-lockup-transparent.svg`
- Light headers: `svg/nodetrail-lockup-ink-transparent.svg`
- One-color work: the mono white or mono black mark and lockups
- Profile and server images: the padded avatars in `avatars/` (X, Telegram, Discord, GitHub, and the Telegram bot)

A one-page sheet of the kit is in `kit-sheet.png`.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark of about one stem width. Do not let type, other marks, or a crop cut into that space.
- Use the glow coin on any background. It has its own dark field.
- Use the flat color mark on mid-to-dark or cream fields where the gradient stays readable.
- Use mono white on dark or busy color. Use mono black on light paper.
- The trail line down the centre of the N is only for 48 px and up. At 16 px and 32 px, use the files already in this kit. Do not redraw the mark by hand at small sizes.

## Do not

- Do not add a T into the N
- Do not rotate, outline, recolor, or drop a shadow on the mark
- Do not change the official colors
- Do not put the color mark on a competing rainbow, or on a field that clashes with the orange, gold, or blue in the mark
- Do not use any other file as the official mark
